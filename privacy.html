Privacy, by design.

PRIVACY POLICY · LAST UPDATED NOVEMBER 19, 2026

ShortsAway (“we,” “our,” or “the app”) is a privacy-first, offline digital wellbeing Android utility designed to help users curb compulsive scrolling by selectively blocking short-form video feeds. Your personal data belongs to you. This policy explains our zero-data-collection design and our use of Android system services.

In short: ShortsAway does not have internet access. It does not collect or transmit personal data. Your app data stays on your device.

1. Offline architecture and data collection

  • No internet permission: ShortsAway does not declare or request the android.permission.INTERNET permission. The app cannot transmit data over the internet or establish network connections.
  • No analytics or telemetry: The app does not include analytics frameworks, crash reporters, tracking SDKs, or third-party advertising identifiers.
  • No accounts: You do not need to register or provide an email address or phone number to use ShortsAway.

2. On-device data storage

Data created or changed in ShortsAway is stored in your device’s private app storage using local SQLite (Room Database) and Jetpack DataStore. This includes:

  • Daily statistics, such as short-form videos closed, daily minutes, and focus streaks.
  • Your preferences, including daily allowance, focus schedule, and platform toggles.
  • If enabled, your four-digit PIN is hashed with SHA-256 and stored on your device. Biometric authentication uses Android’s BiometricPrompt API; biometric credentials do not leave the Android Secure Enclave or Keystore.

Local app data is permanently deleted when you uninstall ShortsAway or clear its data in Android Settings.

3. Android AccessibilityService

ShortsAway uses Android’s AccessibilityService API only to provide its digital wellbeing and impulse-control features.

Purpose and actions

The service detects when supported short-form video player containers enter the active viewport in apps you select. It can dispatch a system Back action (GLOBAL_ACTION_BACK) or display a mindful breathing pause.

What the service does not do

  • It does not read, capture, store, or log typed text, passwords, usernames, or keystrokes.
  • It does not inspect messages, chats, email, banking information, or photos.
  • It does not transmit screen content or metadata off your device.
  • It does not modify device settings or interfere with other app features outside supported short-form feeds.

The service is configured with android:isAccessibilityTool="false". ShortsAway shows a full-screen disclosure before requesting accessibility permission. You can pause or disable the service in the app, using the Quick Settings tile, or in Android System Settings.

4. Children’s privacy

ShortsAway does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

5. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here and included in future app releases. Updates will uphold ShortsAway’s offline design and zero-data-collection commitment.

6. Contact

For questions or feedback, open an issue in the ShortsAway GitHub repository.
